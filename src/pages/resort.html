About Primošten Resort

Primošten Resort is a new development of apartments and villas on the slopes above Primošten, one of the most sought-after small towns on the Dalmatian coast. Its Sunhill settlement is made up of ten buildings, set among the hills with views over the sea and the old town.

The beaches are 600 to 700 metres away, Primošten's old town is a short drive, and Split Airport is about 40 km away. Šibenik is 30 km to the north.

Tomislav Plenča is a sales agent for the resort. He can show you the buildings, explain the floor plans and finishes, and tell you which units are still available.
