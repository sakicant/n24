# Homepage surface brief

Scope: homepage `/` of N24 Real Estate. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: buyers (local or foreign) looking for property between Vodice and Rogoznica, often on phones from a Google search. Primary action: find a listing (area / type) and contact Tomislav (enquiry, call, WhatsApp). Secondary: owners to "Sell with N24"; services teaser. Content: 8 real listings from the current site (content/source-photos), service names, contact facts. No testimonials or stats may be invented. User asked to proceed without further questions (2026-10-22).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The coast is the search. The 8 areas lie in exact order on the D8 coast road (Vodice, Srima, Šibenik, Brodarica, Žaborić, Grebaštica, Primošten, Rogoznica); the homepage draws that road as a strip map and every stop filters the listings. Refuses the category default of a photo hero with a floating search box and a featured carousel.

OWN-WORLD: Sand ground #F6F3EE, navy #13264B line work and headings, ink #1A1A1A body, red #D2252E only for what matters now (selected stop, price, the primary action, the logo frame). Coastline drawn as a single navy line with km-stone markers: small square stops echoing the logo frame; a selected stop becomes a red frame. Cormorant Garamond display (the logo's wordmark face), Manrope text, tabular figures for prices, km and m². Square corners, hairline rules, photography full-bleed inside square frames.

STORY: Visitor sees the sea and knows this is property on the Šibenik coast; sees all 8 places they searched for on one road with live counts; taps one, sees its listings; opens a listing or messages Tomislav. Owners see "Sell with N24". Investors see land and services.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (logo left, Buy/Rent/Sell/Areas/Primošten Resort/Services/Contact, WhatsApp button red right). Headline "Property on the Šibenik coast, from Vodice to Rogoznica" in Cormorant, large, left on sand, over a wide sea photo band. Below/overlapping the photo: the coast-road strip edge to edge, 8 stops with names and counts, type chips (All, Houses, Apartments, Villas, Land, New builds) and a navy "Show N properties" action. On phones the strip becomes a horizontal coast ribbon with snap scrolling.

FORM: Jadranska magistrala strip map with km stones; position 7 on the ordered grounded list; seed key fe047a31. Raises: red only for what matters now (from glazier); one horizon band as the page spine (from indoor sun); tabular figures for distances and prices (from detector). Signature interaction: selecting a stop slides the red frame along the road and the listing grid filters in place with a short crossfade, URL updated (?area=primosten&type=land).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
