About N24

A Šibenik real estate agency since 2010, working the coast between Split and Zadar, with one person you can always reach: Tomislav Plenča.

Tomislav's photo

Tomislav Plenča

Director and sales lead

Tomislav runs N24 and leads every sale himself. Buyers and owners deal with him directly, from the first viewing to the contract at the notary, so nothing gets lost between agents.

Much of his work happens before a property is ever advertised: checking ownership in the land registry and cadastre, finding owners and heirs of fragmented plots, and putting together land that investors can actually build on.
