    Living in Vodice

    Vodice is a busy seaside town about 12 km north-west of Šibenik. In summer it is one of the most popular places on the riviera, with a long promenade, beaches, cafés and the ACI marina. Out of season it is a working town with shops, schools and a health centre, so it suits both holiday owners and people who live here all year.

    The ferry and boats from Vodice reach the islands of Prvić and Tijat, and the Kornati islands are a popular day trip from the harbour.

    Houses, villas and apartments in Vodice

    Most properties in Vodice are apartments in small residential buildings, family houses with an apartment to rent, and new builds close to the sea. Apartments are a common choice for buyers who want rental income in summer.

    Building land in Vodice

    Building plots in Vodice are mostly on the edges of town and on the hills behind it, many with a sea view. Before buying, it is essential to check that a plot lies inside the building zone of the spatial plan and has road access and utilities.

    Buying property in Vodice: questions

    Is Vodice good for rental income?

    Vodice has one of the longest tourist seasons on the Šibenik riviera, so apartments close to the sea and the centre usually rent well in summer. Tomislav can tell you what similar properties earn and what to watch for.

    Can foreigners buy property in Vodice?

    Citizens of EU countries can buy property in Croatia under the same conditions as Croatian citizens. For citizens of other countries it depends on an agreement between Croatia and their country and on approval by the Ministry of Justice. Tomislav will check your case before you make an offer.

    What should I check before buying land in Vodice?

    Check that the plot lies inside the building zone of the spatial plan, that the land registry and the cadastre match, who all the owners are, and that the plot has road access and utilities. N24 checks all of this for the land it sells.
