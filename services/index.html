Land consolidation

Turning many small plots with many owners into one plot you can build on.

Along the Croatian coast, land is usually split into many small cadastral plots, often with owners who live abroad, owners who have died, or heirs nobody has traced. That is the main reason good building land stays stuck. N24 analyses the land, checks every record and brings the owners together, so investors can decide with real facts and buy one clean plot.

How land consolidation works

  1. Site and plot analysisWhat is on the ground, what the spatial plan allows, and which plots belong together.
  2. Cadastre and land registryOwnership, boundaries, burdens and mismatches between the two registers.
  3. Owners and heirsContacting every owner, and finding the missing ones and their heirs.
  4. Offers and purchasePrice offers, negotiation and contracts with each owner.
  5. One plotA consolidated plot, ready for the next step: design and permit.

Investment consulting

The numbers behind a purchase, before you commit.

If you want to know what a property or a plot is really worth, and what it could earn, N24 works through the figures with you. We look for short, medium and long-term opportunities on the market and bring properties to the market with added value, for buyers who want to understand their investment, not only see it.

  • Market prices and comparable sales in the area
