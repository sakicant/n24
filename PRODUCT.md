# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Phase 1 (now): frontend only. Static HTML/CSS/JS built with a small Node
build script (same approach as the owner's other sites), previewed on Vercel
with sample listing data. Phase 2: PHP + MySQL backend (listings database,
admin panel, enquiry and sell forms) on the same LiteSpeed/Apache host as
leilasibenik.com, but in its own folder with its own PHP and its own
database. Fully separate from Leila: no shared code, data or config.

## Users

Buyers, local or foreign, looking for property on the Šibenik coast between
Split and Zadar: a house, villa, apartment, new build or a plot of land. The
homepage speaks to buyers first. Secondary: property owners who want N24 to
sell for them; investors who need land consolidation or project management.

## Product Purpose

The website of Nekretnine 24 d.o.o. (N24), a Šibenik real estate agency
since 2010. It lets buyers find and enquire about listings, sends owners to
a "sell with N24" form, and presents N24's services. Success: buyers find a
relevant listing within a few clicks and contact Tomislav by enquiry form,
call or WhatsApp.

## Positioning

A local agency, not a portal: one director who knows every listing, 15+
years on this stretch of coast, and specialist land work (plot
consolidation, cadastral and ownership analysis, finding heirs) that
portals and generalist agents do not do. Official sales channel of
Tomislav for Primošten Resort.

## Operating Context

- About 100 active listings; Primošten Resort alone has 100+ units.
- Listings are published only on this site, managed in a custom admin panel.
- Visitors arrive mostly from Google searches like "real estate Primošten",
  "land for sale Vodice", often on phones.
- English first; Croatian and German later.

## Product Principles

1. Buyers first: every page leads to listings or to a conversation with Tomislav.
2. Few clicks by default, depth on demand.
3. Local knowledge is the proof: areas, land, real distances, real prices.
4. One listing, entered once, appears everywhere it belongs.
5. Fast and clear on a phone.
