    Living in Grebaštica

    Grebaštica is a village about 19 km south of Šibenik, set around a long, sheltered bay. The bay keeps the water calm, which makes it popular with swimmers and small-boat owners.

    It is a quiet place, with Primošten and Šibenik both a short drive away along the coast road.

    Houses, villas and apartments in Grebaštica

    In Grebaštica you will mostly find family houses and holiday houses, many of them close to the bay, and a smaller number of villas.

    Building land in Grebaštica

    Building plots around the bay and on the slopes above it come up from time to time. Many are made of several older cadastral plots, which is where N24's land consolidation work helps.

    Buying property in Grebaštica: questions

    Is Grebaštica good for people with a boat?

    The sheltered bay makes Grebaštica one of the calmer spots on this coast for small boats. Ask Tomislav about properties close to the water and about berths in the area.

    Can foreigners buy property in Grebaštica?

    Citizens of EU countries can buy property in Croatia under the same conditions as Croatian citizens. For citizens of other countries it depends on an agreement between Croatia and their country and on approval by the Ministry of Justice. Tomislav will check your case before you make an offer.

    What should I check before buying land in Grebaštica?

    Check that the plot lies inside the building zone of the spatial plan, that the land registry and the cadastre match, who all the owners are, and that the plot has road access and utilities. N24 checks all of this for the land it sells.
